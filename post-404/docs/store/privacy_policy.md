# Privacy Policy — POST 404: Parcels From Tomorrow

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

POST 404 does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

- The game works fully offline and does not request internet access.
- Your progress and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
- The game contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- The only permission requested is vibration (haptic feedback), which can be turned off in Settings.

If this policy changes in a future version (for example, if optional purchases are added), the change will be
published here before that version is released.

Contact: the developer via the Google Play store page.
