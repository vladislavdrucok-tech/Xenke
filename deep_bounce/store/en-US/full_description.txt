Swipe, release — your cannon fires a stream of balls down the shaft. They ricochet off the walls and stones, and every number is a stone's strength. After each volley the rock rises one row: keep it away from the red line under the cannon.

• Endless Mode: dig as deep as you can and beat your record.
• 50 handcrafted levels in 5 chapters — Copper Mine, Crystal Vein, Flooded Tunnels, Obsidian Depths and the Heart of the Mine. Earn up to 3 stars for clearing a level in few volleys.
• Upgrades every few volleys: more balls, heavy balls that smash strong stones, electric balls that spark the nearest stone.
• Real ricochet physics: the aim line shows exactly where the first rebound goes.
• Crystals from broken stones unlock ball and cannon finishes. Finishes change the look only, never damage or speed.
• One second chance per run, doubled crystals after a run — with a short video, or without one in the Ad-Free pack.
• Plays offline. Your run is saved at any moment, even mid-volley.
• 12 languages.

The game is free to play with optional ads and optional in-app purchases (Ad-Free, cosmetic finishes, support for the developer).
