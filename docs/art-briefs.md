# v0.2 art assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the earlier Afterfall concept as a style/character reference. The production files below are encoded as WebP with alpha preserved on the atlases. The runtime trims each atlas cell once and uses the results for actual animations and object rendering.

## Environment

Path: `dist/assets/expedition-world.webp`.

Prompt: a production background plate for a playable 2D side-scrolling survival game, not a screenshot or poster; richly detailed pixel art of an overgrown post-apocalyptic city, amber lamps, teal shadows, tactile brick, rusted metal, small foliage and atmospheric depth. No characters, animals, UI, text or logo. Extra-wide 3:1 panorama. Flat pavement at 78% image height, cutaway soil below. Left: a ground-floor cutaway shelter workshop with greenhouse and water tank. Center: abandoned street, vegetation and a safe stream crossing. Right: a damaged botanical institute and seed repository. Distant damaged skyscrapers in lavender/orange dusk haze. Orthographic side view, crisp pixel clusters, continuous walkable path.

## Character animation

Path: `dist/assets/einstein-atlas.webp`.

Prompt: a production transparent sprite sheet, exactly eight full-body sprites of the same caricature Albert Einstein survivor, strict four-column/two-row grid, equal cells, no labels/grid/background. Right-facing orthographic side view, uniform scale and baseline. Wild white hair and moustache, brown jacket, teal scarf, olive backpack, dark utility trousers, leather boots and small tools. Crisp detailed pixel clusters. Top row: four consecutive walk poses. Bottom row: idle with pickaxe, pickaxe raised, pickaxe striking forward, jump. Full tools contained within each cell with padding.

## Objects and creatures

Path: `dist/assets/objects-atlas.webp`.

Prompt: a production transparent 2D pixel-art object atlas, strict four-column/two-row grid, no text or scenery. Orthographic side-view objects fully inside their cells. In reading order: harvestable stump and logs; orange ore rock; salvage crate with cloth and gears; olive seed case with a leaf emblem; handmade blue-barrel water filter; detailed workbench and lamp; friendly mutated fox with leaf ears and fern tail; six-legged armored burrowing mutant. Fine crisp pixel clusters, warm highlights and dark teal shading consistent with the Afterfall reference.

These are usable atlas illustrations for this prototype; production animation polish and more poses remain future work.
